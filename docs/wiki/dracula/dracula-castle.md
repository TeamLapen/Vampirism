Dracula's Castle is the only structure in [Velmorra](./velmorra). It is a huge castle roughly 270 blocks wide with several floors, a dungeon and caves below. It stands at the center of the realm and is identical in every Velmorra; only the loot is rolled randomly.

## Rooms and loot

Chests and barrels in the castle use their own loot tables, depending on the room:

- kitchen and pantry (food)
- library
- bedrooms
- guard towers
- dungeon and caves
- a hunter camp

Some rooms also contain villagers, including librarians.

## The Chalices

Four **Chalices** stand in the castle. Dracula is summoned as soon as at least four Chalices in Velmorra are filled.

Fill a Chalice by right-clicking it with one of these:

- Vampire Blood Bottle
- any Pure Blood
- Sovereign Blood
- a completely full Blood Bottle

Each Chalice takes one item. An empty Chalice only makes an echoing sound.

:::caution
The fight starts as soon as the fourth Chalice is filled and the Chalices are emptied again. Make sure everybody is ready before you fill the last one.
:::

When the fight starts, Dracula appears at one of several spawn points in the castle and the message *"The scent of blood awakens the master of the mansion"* is shown. See [the fight](./dracula-fight).

## After the fight

When Dracula dies, a return portal is built at the arrival point and the [collapse](./velmorra#collapse) begins. Use the remaining ten minutes to loot the castle.
