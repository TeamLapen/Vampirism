The endgame of Vampirism is the fight against **Dracula** in the **Velmorra** dimension.

1. Find a [Velmorra Portal](./portal-activation) in the overworld and activate it with the three **Ritual Knives**. Only lords of the highest lord level can do this.
2. Travel to [Velmorra](./velmorra) and make your way to [Dracula's Castle](./dracula-castle).
3. Fill the four Chalices in the castle with blood to summon Dracula.
4. Defeat him in the [three-phase fight](./dracula-fight).
5. Leave through the return portal before Velmorra collapses.

## Rewards

Every player that took part in the fight (everybody who was in Velmorra while the fight was running) gets:

- the advancement **The King is Dead**,
- 500 experience points,
- and, if they are a vampire lord of the highest lord level, the rank **Dracula** (see [Vampire Skills](../factions/vampire/skills#dracula-skills)).

Dracula himself drops a large amount of Sovereign Blood.

## Tips

- Play in a group. Dracula's health grows with every additional player in Velmorra, and so does the number of minions and bats he summons.
- The only exit from Velmorra is the return portal that opens when Dracula is dead (or the portal you came through, as long as it is open).
