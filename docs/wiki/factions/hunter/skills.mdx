Hunters have three skill trees, opened with the skill screen (faction menu, `P` by default): **Level Skills**, **Lord Skills** and, for the Marshall only, **Marshall Skills**.  
The costs below are skill points. Many hunter items and workstations need a skill before you can craft or use them. Missing skills are marked red in the item tooltip.

## Lord Skills

Available once you are a Hunter Lord.

<SkillOrAction title="Becoming a Hunter Lord" iconPath={`${tex}skills/hunter_lord_root.png`}>
The root of the Lord tree.

</SkillOrAction>
<SkillOrAction title="Better Minions" iconPath={`${tex}skills/hunter_better_minions.png`}>
Cost: 3. Improve the stats of your minions.

</SkillOrAction>
<SkillOrAction title="Minion Technology" iconPath={`${tex}skills/minion_technology.png`}>
Cost: 1. Allow your minions to wield semi-auto crossbows.

</SkillOrAction>

Further Lord skills come from shared lord skills and FactionApi:

- **Lord Movement Speed / Lord Attack Speed** (choose one): boost the movement speed or attack speed of nearby faction members (action).
- **Resource Collection**: send your minions out to collect resources.
- **Resource Diversity**: send your minions out to collect specific kinds of resources. Better tools yield better resources.
- **Minion Recovery**: speeds up the time your minions need to recover.

## Marshall Skills

The Marshall is the top hunter rank, see [Marshall](./hunter-player#marshall). It has its own tree, paid for with research points instead of skill points.
