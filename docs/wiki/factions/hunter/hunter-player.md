## Traits
* Hunters have levels (1 to 14) and, as Hunter Lords, lord levels
* Hunter NPCs (trainers and village hunters) cure the Sanguinare Vampiris infection when you interact with them
* Hunters are specialized in weapons, armor and other equipment. Most of it needs a [skill](./skills) (and some a minimum level) before you can craft or use it. The tooltip lists the *Required Skills* and *Required Level* in red if you miss them (hold the item description key to see more).
* Can use stakes to e.g., instantly kill vampires below 35% health (skill *How to Use a Stake*)
* Vampire NPCs killed with a stake drop Vampire Blood, used for leveling and for brewing at the Vapor Still

## Equipment

### Weapons
* **Hunter Axe**: normal, enhanced and ultimate versions need hunter level 4, 9 and 14. With the skill *How to Actually Use an Axe* it makes vampires bleed.
* **Stake**: little damage, but extracts Vampire Blood from vampires. *How to Use a Stake* and *How to Actually Use a Stake* upgrade it.
* **Crossbows**: basic, enhanced and semi-auto crossbows, plus double crossbows. They need *Basic Technology*, *Master Craftsmanship* or *Artisan Craftsmanship* depending on the version (basic/enhanced/ultimate tier); double crossbows additionally need *Double It*. *Dual Wielding* lets you wield two crossbows.
* **Crucifixes**: Wooden, Iron and Golden. Hold right-click to slow vampires and undead approaching you; affected vampires are briefly blocked from using their powers. Wooden and Iron need *Crucifix Wielder*, the Golden one *Crucifixion*. *Crucifix Repelling* adds a right-click knockback.
* **Oils**: applied to weapons, armor or pickaxes for special effects; the tooltip shows where an oil can be used and how many uses are left.

### Crossbow ammunition
"Crossbow arrows" are called **Quarrels** now. Types: Quarrel, Heavy, Bleeding, Garlic, Spitfire, Teleport and Vampire Killer. The Heavy Quarrel deals more damage and knockback but drops faster and takes longer to charge and loose.
* **Quarrel Clip** contains 16 quarrels, the **Heavy Quarrel Clip** 8 heavy quarrels.
* **Quarrel Pouch** holds several stacks of quarrels for quick reloading. Press the *Select Quarrel Type* key (`C` by default) to choose the type.
* **Precision** enchantment: improves accuracy when shooting a hunter crossbow and reduces quarrel drop.

### Armor
* **Hunter Coat** (replaces the Obsidian Armor, normal/enhanced/ultimate) and **Armor of Swiftness** need *Basic Technology*, *Master Craftsmanship* or *Artisan Craftsmanship* by tier.
* **Hunter Hat** comes in a *Tall* and a *Broad* variant and needs no skill.
* *Armor-Bound Swiftness* and *Armor-Bound Highvault* give speed / jump bonuses in a full set of hunter armor.
* *Near-Breach Reforging*: armor does not break but turns into **Shattered Armor**, which can be repaired at an anvil.

### Workstations
* **Weapon Table**: crafts advanced weapons including crossbows. Needs *Basic Technology*. Some recipes require lava, so fill the table with a lava bucket by right-clicking it. JEI shows the recipes and the skills they need.
* **Alchemical Cauldron**: needs *Apprentice Alchemy*.
* **Vapor Still** (was Potion Table / Blood Potion Table): brew hunter potions with Vampire Blood. The brewing skills (*Multitask*, *Concentrated*, *Durable*, *Swift*, *Efficient*, *Forgotten Formulary*) unlock more potions, faster brewing and larger batches.
* **Hunter Table** (Hunter Research Table): turns books, vampire fangs, pure blood and vampire books into the Hunter Intel needed to level up, see [Leveling](./leveling).
* **Garlic Diffusers** (were Garlic Beacons): normal, strong and long versions repel vampires in an area. They need *Vampire Repelling*; Purified Garlic needs *Refined Garlic* and the strong/long diffuser cores *Augmented Diffusers*.

### Injections
* **Empty Syringe** (was Syringe): craft injections with it or take blood from mobs.
* **Garlic Injection** (Empty Syringe + garlic) and **Sanguinare Injection** (Empty Syringe + 8 vampire fangs) are used at an **Injection Chair** (was Medical Chair).
* **Serum Injection**: inject a potion effect directly. Some potions are blocked, including hunter potions.

### Other
* **Golden Heart** and **Enchanted Golden Heart**: food items. Vampires get Regeneration and Absorption (the enchanted one also Resistance and Fire Resistance), everyone else gets Nausea.

## Marshall
The Marshall is a special top hunter rank. Marshalls have their own skill tree (see [Skills](./skills#marshall-skills)) that uses research points. The skills allow imbuing items and potions with Sovereign Blood.  
In this version the only way to become a Marshall is the command `/factions faction set vampirism:hunter marshall [players]` (game master permission). It sets the player to the highest level and lord level; the rank is lost if the level or lord level drops below the maximum.

## Stop being a Hunter
If you want to leave the hunters, craft a Sanguinare Injection and use it at an Injection Chair. A menu asks you to confirm: you lose all your levels and die.
