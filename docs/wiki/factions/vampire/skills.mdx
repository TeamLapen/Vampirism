Main article [here](../skills)

Skills are unlocked in the skill screen (hold-click). The tree is split into three parts: the level tree, the [lord tree](#lord-skills) and the [Dracula tree](#dracula-skills).
All branches start at the root and run through Night Vision, Regeneration and No Longer a Fledgling.
If a skill is the only one you can unlock, it is unlocked automatically on level-up.

## Dracula Skills

This separate tree is only available to players with the **Dracula** rank, which is earned by defeating Dracula (see [Dracula](../../dracula/intro)). Its skills are bought with Dracula marks instead of skill points.

<VSkill title="Becoming Dracula" icon="dracula_root.png">

You are now Dracula, with wings

</VSkill>
<VSkill title="Mist" icon="mist.png">

<UnlockAction action="Mist"/>

Shift into mist to evade. While in mist form you move faster, fall slower and jump lower, and sunlight does not affect you.

</VSkill>
<VSkill title="Aura of Darkness" icon="aura_of_darkness.png">

<UnlockAction action="Aura of Darkness"/>

Darken the nearby area to stop the sun from reaching through. Also protects vampires close to you.

</VSkill>
