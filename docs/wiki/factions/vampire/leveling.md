After becoming a vampire (see [Getting Started](../getting-started#first-steps---vampire)) you probably want to reach higher levels.

## Passive Leveling (To reach level 2-3)
You do not stay a fledgling forever: by drinking blood from living creatures you level up on your own up to level 3.
This needs a lot of blood and is slow. A progress bar is shown in the faction menu.
The amount of blood needed can be scaled in the balance config (`passiveLevelingBloodFactor`), and the server can turn passive leveling off (`passiveLevelingEnabled`), in which case you need the Altar of Inspiration from level 2 on.
Blood from bottles and containers does not count, only blood sucked from creatures.

## Altars
Beyond passive leveling you need rituals at altars. The Altar of Infusion only works **at night**.
Altar items are only consumed when you actually level up.

## Altar of Inspiration (To reach level 2-4)
Craft an Altar of Inspiration and place it somewhere.
To use it, fill it with blood (right-click with filled blood containers). If there is enough blood inside, right-click it with an empty hand and the blood will be infused into you, making you even stronger.  
_Required blood amount: level 2: 40, level 3: 70, level 4: 100_  

## Altar of Infusion (To reach level 5+)
This altar requires a multi-block structure. First place the main block (Altar of Infusion), then build Altar Pillars around it and fill them with special blocks (right click them while holding the block). Each pillar needs an Altar Tip on top. Make sure there are no other blocks (e.g. grass) in the area. After the structure is complete, put the required items into the altar (open it with sneak + right click), right-click the altar and wait for the magic to happen.
If something is missing the altar tells you (wrong purity, missing pure blood / human hearts / vampire books, missing pillars, not night).

#### Structure Points
The required structure depends on the level, you need a specific amount of "structure points".
The value of each pillar depends on the block inside: Stone Bricks: 1, Bone Block: 1.5, Iron Block: 2, Gold Block: 3.
A pillar has a maximum height of 3 filled blocks, and at most 9 pillars count.

## Leaving the Faction
If you no longer want to be a vampire you need to find or craft an Altar of Cleansing and interact with it.
There you will die to leave the faction unless you hold a Totem of Undying or play in a hardcore world.
