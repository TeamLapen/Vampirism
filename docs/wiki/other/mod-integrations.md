---

Vampirism 2.0 requires the separate mod **FactionApi** and optionally supports:
- [JEI](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/jei) (recipe categories, e.g. Blood Grinder, Blood Sieve, Blood Infuser, Vapor Still)
- Guide-API-VP (the Vampirism guide book)
- TerraBlender

Further integrations are outsourced to a second mod [VampirismIntegrations](https://github.com/TeamLapen/VampirismIntegrations) [Download](https://minecraft.curseforge.com/projects/vampirism-integrations/files).  
**VampirismIntegrations for Minecraft 1.21 is not compatible with Vampirism 2.0** and has to be ported first.

For a list of implemented integrations head over to the Integrations [wiki](https://github.com/TeamLapen/VampirismIntegrations/wiki).
