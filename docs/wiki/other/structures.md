## Important structures
There are new structures generated in the world.
Here is a list of the most important ones:

### Village structures

#### Hunter Trainer house
Generated in some villages.  
Has some garlic growing outside.  
Home of a Hunter Trainer. In villages that are not controlled by hunters the trainer is an undercover trainer, see [Village Life](../factions/village/village-life#hunter-trainer).

#### Village Totem
Generated in some villages. Controls the [faction of the village](../factions/village/controlling).

#### Church
Generated in some villages (a part of the village temples is replaced, server option `replaceVillageTemples`).  
Looks like a normal church. 
Has an Altar of Cleansing which can be used to cure you of vampirism

Trainer houses and totems are added to the vanilla village types; for other village types a mod like Repurposed Structures is used (Vampirism ships data for it).

### Hunter structures

#### Hunter Outpost
A fortified outpost with towers, walls and hunters (`Hunter` and `Advanced Hunter`) living there. The towers contain chests with hunter gear, food and ingredients.  
There are four variants, each in a different biome type: plains/forests, desert, badlands and the Vampire Forest.  
Chests can contain Potions of Oblivion, crossbows, hunter axes, hunter coats, garlic and vampire books. The outpost with the special tower can contain ultimate hunter gear.

#### Hunter Camp / Tent
A small camp with a fireplace and a tent, generated in plains, desert, meadow, snowy plains, sparse jungle, forests, taigas and badlands (but not in the pale garden or the Vampire Forest) and not close to villages. The tent can be used to set your respawn point (hunters).  
Can be disabled by the server option `generateHunterCamps`.

### Vampire structures

#### Crypt
Generated in the Vampire Forest. A church above ground with stairs and corridors leading down to rooms with coffins.  
Chests contain for example Heartseeker/Heartstriker, amulets, rings, vampire books, blood bottles and sometimes a Potion of Oblivion. A chest can contain a map to the Ancient Remains.  
Entering one grants the advancement *Domain of The Dead*. The Hunter Expert and Vampire Expert villagers sell maps to crypts.

#### Vampire Hut
A small hut in the Vampire Forest. The chest contains vampire items like amulets, rings, fangs, blood bottles and vampire books.

#### Vampire Altar
A small altar with a chest and an Advanced Vampire guarding it. Generates in wastelands, plateaus, rare, spooky and swamp biomes (so also in the Vampire Forest). The chest contains pure blood, blood bottles, fangs and ingots.

#### Ancient Remains
Generated in the Vampire Forest, far apart from each other. Defeating the *Ancient Remains* grants the advancements *Ancient Dominion* (vampire) / *Hunter of the Shadows* (hunter). A map to it can be found in crypt chests (*Reopening Old Wounds*).

### Dracula structures

#### Velmorra Portal
Generated rarely in the overworld. Used to travel to Velmorra, see [Portal Activation](../dracula/portal-activation).

#### Dracula's Castle
Generated in the dimension Velmorra, see [Dracula's Castle](../dracula/dracula-castle).
