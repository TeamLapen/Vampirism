## Headline changes

### Velmorra and the Dracula fight

A complete end-game questline has been added.

- **Velmorra** — a new dimension: Dracula's crumbling realm, reached through a
  **Velmorra Portal** structure found in the world.
- Activate the portal at the **Velmorra Altar** by offering blood. Weak blood is refused —
  the portal only reacts to sufficiently pure blood.
- Drinking **Pure Blood** grants *Whispers of the Veil*, which points you toward the
  nearest Velmorra Portal.
- Inside Velmorra stands **Dracula's castle**, a large multi-part structure with a throne
  room, paintings and subsurface sections.
- Fill four **Chalices** with blood to summon **Dracula**. The fight runs in three phases
  with a ranged phase, a raged phase, a bat-swarm dash, a blood-siphon channel and a mist
  form, plus full-screen visual effects.
- When Dracula dies, Velmorra begins to collapse. You get ten minutes to reach the return portal that opens in
  the castle before the realm consumes itself.
- Rewards: the **The King is Dead** advancement, a large XP payout, and for max-level
  lords the new end-game skill trees below.

### FactionApi is now a separate mod

Everything generic about factions — factions themselves, levels, lord levels, skills,
actions, minions, tasks, village capture/totems and raids — now lives in **FactionApi**,
a standalone mod with its own ID (`factionapi`), its own config files and its own
CurseForge/Modrinth pages. Vampirism depends on it.

For players this mostly means:
- The FactionApi mod needs to be installed.
- Faction settings moved from `vampirism-*.toml` into `factionapi-*.toml`.
- Other mods can now add factions without depending on Vampirism.

A **neutral faction** was added, so non-vampire/non-hunter players are properly modelled
rather than being a special case.

### Skill trees rebuilt

The old "skill node" layout has been replaced by a **skill segment** system.

- Segments can have multiple parents, choice branches (*this or that*) and sibling-relative
  placement, so the trees actually branch instead of being a chain of nodes.
- Skills now require **holding** the button to unlock, and connection lines render
  correctly for multi-parent and unlocked/locked skills.
- Skill titles no longer get cut off.
- Every tree has its own **skill point type**, so hunter research points, vampire skill
  points, dracula marks and marshall research points are kept apart.
- If only one skill is unlockable on level up, it is unlocked automatically.
- Two new end-game trees unlock after beating Dracula:
    - **Becoming Dracula** (vampire) — become Dracula yourself, with wings.
    - **Marshall** (hunter) — the hunter counterpart.

The hunter tree was rebuilt (39 segments) and the vampire tree reorganised (32 segments),
with reordered weapon lines and adjusted costs.

## Vampires

- **Passive leveling** — you can now grow from level 1 to 3 just by drinking blood from
  living creatures, without an altar. It is slower and costs more blood, and levels beyond
  3 still require the Altar of Infusion. There is a progress bar in the faction menu and
  server config to disable it or tune the blood cost.
- **Sun blinding** — looking directly at the sun as a vampire washes out the screen, with
  lens flares and bloom that scale with your level. Configurable intensity, an optional
  black variant, and it can be kept or hidden while Iris shaders are active.
- Vampires now visually **catch fire** when burning in the sun, and spawn ash particles
  when unprotected.
- **Sanguinare Vampiris** now has visible stages: hunger, then *Sun Sensitivity*, then
  *Exposed*, with occasional nausea as the infection progresses.
- New actions:
    - **Mist Form** — shift into mist to evade, with a dedicated mist renderer.
    - **Aura of Darkness** — darken the area around you so the sun cannot reach through,
      with a screen-space shader effect.
    - **Dark Stalker** — move completely silently.
- **Blood Vision** was reworked and now fades out for entities very close to you.
- Feeding on a sleeping villager no longer costs reputation.
- Infecting non-players and non-villagers no longer requires standing behind them.
- Bat form: smaller, thinner hitbox matching the player model; item use is blocked while
  in bat form or downed; wings are disabled in bat mode.
- **Heart Seeker / Heart Striker** are now tiered by material — **iron, diamond,
  netherite** — instead of normal/enhanced/ultimate.

## Hunters

The hunter side received the largest single rework.

- **Quarrels replace crossbow arrows.** Crossbows now fire dedicated quarrels:
    - Normal, **Heavy**, Spitfire, Garlic, Vampire Killer, Teleport and **Bleeding**.
    - Heavy quarrels have real ballistics: more damage, more knockback and extra piercing,
      but slower flight and faster drop. Fired in an arc, they gain damage the further they
      *fall* from the peak of their trajectory.
    - Quarrels can be **oiled** for on-impact effects.
- **Quarrel Pouch** — holds several stacks of quarrels and reloads directly from it.
  **Quarrel Clips** and **Heavy Quarrel Clips** for the tech crossbow.
- New **ammo selection keybind** for choosing what to load.
- Crossbows gained inaccuracy handling, and now accept **Piercing** and **Quick Charge**.
  A new **Precision** enchantment was added; **Arrow Frugality** is now restricted to tech
  crossbows.
- Crossbow and armour tiers are now **gated by skills** instead of being freely craftable.
- **Serum Injections** replace the old zombie-blood injection line: brewed from potions,
  injected with a syringe, with JEI support and a hunter skill to combine two potions into
  one injection. A `serum_blocked` tag replaces the old config list.
- **Blood Syringe** and a reworked **Empty Syringe** (now requires holding to use).
- **Vapor Still** replaces the potion table — remodelled, with proper JEI integration,
  a fixed ingredient hopper and a one-item potion slot that accepts any stack size.
- **Garlic Diffusers** became furnace-like blocks with three tiers (normal/long/strong)
  plus matching cores, a boot-up phase, fuel handling, owner locking and a lit state.
  They reveal the aura of nearby diffusers and their cores.
- **Crucifix Repel** skill and a **Crucifix Suppression** effect that disables vampire
  actions on hit.
- Hunter hats: **Broad** and **Tall** variants with separate names; advanced hunters can
  use any item as headwear.
- New hunter textures throughout, an updated disguise texture, and new hunter intel model.
- Hunters can walk on powder snow, defend their comrades, and open gates (hunter minions
  cannot).

## Blood, brewing and crafting

- **Blood Infuser** — a new block. Infuse weapons with **Pure Blood** (and, late game,
  the **Blood of Dracula**) to upgrade them. Comes with its own recipe type, recipe book
  category and JEI category.
- **Blood is now a pourable fluid** with corrected flow speed and thickness, and block
  entities render their contained fluid correctly.
- **Blood Grinder** remade: must be powered, needs a **Fabric Filter** as consumable,
  has a grinding wheel animation, pouring particles, an internal blood renderer and a new
  squeezing sound. Items can be inserted from any side except the bottom.
- **Blood Sieve** remade and now works with all fluid containers.
- Blood bottles are drunk in one go, no longer return spare blood, and empty blood
  containers stack. Sucking blood works from any container that allows it.
- **Chalice** — a new block that can be filled with any kind of blood.
- **Altar of Infusion** reworked: consumes items only during the actual level-up, handles
  aborts and multiplayer correctly, and its slowness effect is transient and removed when
  broken. The **Altar Pillar** was remodelled, can be glued, and emits an analog redstone
  signal. The **Altar of Cleansing** and **Altar of Inspiration** were remodelled too.
- **Ritual Knife** replaced the charged-knife item with a data component, and a
  **Ritual Knife (Heart)** variant was added.

## World generation

- Vampire forest: a layer of **dark stone** below the cursed earth, **giant dark stone
  roots** beneath it, tall grass near vampire trees, higher vegetation density, and
  vegetation no longer replaces existing blocks.
- **Cursed Roots** added — bonemealing cursed grass grows roots and orchids.
- **Crypt** generation improved; the staircase no longer rotates between pieces and only
  the church piece uses thin terrain adaptation.

## Blocks, decoration and models

- **Candle Sticks, Candelabras and Chandeliers** remade, in all **16 colours**, with wall
  variants, fire particles instead of a texture, and no ambient occlusion. Creative-mode
  placement no longer demands an exact candle count.
- **Coffins** remade, with separate item models.
- **Tombstones** remade and renamed to **Short / Medium / Cross**.
- **Blood Container**, **Bat Cage**, **Blood Pedestal**, **Throne**, **Injection Chair**
  (formerly Med Chair) and the sit entity all remodelled.
- **Vampire Soul Lantern** added, with a new recipe.
- Blood-infused iron block textures updated; a **Refined** variant added.
- The **Ancient Trophy** now spectates players.
- Dark stone brick variants that had no craft got one.
- New cursed earth path textures, updated cobbled dark stone, garlic diffuser models,
  totem models and textures, and many item textures.
- Potted plants and garlic now render in the correct cutout render type.
- The mod's books can be placed into chiseled bookshelves.

## Lore, books and guide

- **Vampire books are now data-driven** — resource-pack and datapack authors can add their
  own, with custom backgrounds and embedded images.
- Many new books: *Ashes of Past Dawns*, *Blood Infusion Breakthrough*, *Moral Distortion*,
  and more, plus a rerolling recipe in case your book came up empty after the update.
- Book authors are credited, book text moved into per-book language files, and a large
  number of books were translated (notably into Ukrainian and Russian).
- Vampire books show the ID of the mod that added them, like enchanted books.
- The **Guidebook** was updated for GuideAPI 3.0 with new entries and tooltips, and is now
  part of the `bookshelf_books` tag.
- **Custom splashes** were added to the main menu, some with special colours.

## Advancements

New: *The King is Dead* (defeat Dracula), *Domain of the Dead*, *Reopening Old Wounds*,
*Patchouli Knowledge*, *Baptism of Blood*, *Texas, 1973*, *Plague Inc.*, *Mainline*,
*Worth It*, *Brochette*, *Crazed Gunman*, and per-crossbow advancements. Several existing
advancement icons, descriptions and orderings were cleaned up, and advancements were added
for all syringe items.

## Quality of life

- An **in-game config screen** for both Vampirism and FactionApi, with a colour picker
  config type.
- Client preferences (action bindings, action order, minion task order) moved into their
  own preference files instead of the config, and reordering menus now save correctly.
- The item-description key is now configurable; HUD overlays hide when the HUD is off.
- New HUD overlays for fangs, the syringe, charged items and nearby vampires.
- Level display now has a level prefix; refinement tooltips show the actual attribute value.
- Faction restriction messages are clearer and mark missing skills in red.
- Item descriptions added for many blocks and items.
- Large language-file reorganisation and a pass over grammar, capitalisation and naming
  across the whole mod.
- Sound files were cleaned up, resampled to 44.1 kHz mono, and sound definitions are now
  data-generated.
- Telemetry collection was **removed**. It was never used anyway.
- and more

## Balance highlights

- Passive leveling is blood-dependent; Death But Not Out is restricted to vampires of
  level 4+.
- Hunter armour is restricted by skills; several hunter skill costs lowered.
- Blood-infused vampire swords rebalanced; base Heart Striker attack speed increased.
- Serum injections rebalanced; the hunter faction restriction on them was removed.
- Oblivion potion spawn chances lowered; crucifix suppression duration slightly reduced.
- Bleeding applies no knockback.
- Config options were added to disable the stake instakill and the sword finisher on
  players, and to blacklist bosses from the vampire sword finisher.

## Removed / renamed

- **Impure Blood** (fluid, bucket) — removed.
- **Fire Place** — removed.
- **Potion Table** → **Vapor Still**. **Med Chair** → **Injection Chair**.
- **Crossbow Arrows** → **Quarrels**. **Tech Crossbow Ammo Package** → **Quarrel Clip**.
- **Castle blocks** → dark stone / purple stone brick and tile families.
- **Garlic Diffuser** (single block) → three tiered diffusers.
- **Tombstone 1/2/3** → Short / Medium / Cross.
- **Totems** and the **Potion of Oblivion** moved to FactionApi.
- NPC **entity actions** were removed; Dracula's actions replaced them with the mist and
  aura-of-darkness actions.
- The **Bivalent Injections** skill was removed. The separate **Vampirism Integrations**
  AppleSkin handling was replaced by a proper integration in FactionApi.
- `itBlockedSerumPotions` config → the `vampirism:serum_blocked` tag.

## Compatibility

- **JEI** integration extended: blood infuser, blood grinder, blood sieve, vapor still,
  garlic/fog diffuser fuels, serum recipes, task display, alchemical cauldron and blessing
  categories.
- **AppleSkin** integration (via FactionApi)
- **TerraBlender** and **GuideAPI** updated

## Migration notes

- Vampirism 2.0 requires **Minecraft 26.1.2** and **FactionApi**.
- Renamed and removed blocks/items mean existing worlds will lose those blocks. Back up
  your world before updating.
